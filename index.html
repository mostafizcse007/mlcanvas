Core Summary

Finds the unique oriented hyperplane that maximizes the geometric margin 2/||w|| separating two classes.

Core Assumption

Data is linearly separable or soft-separable with bounded margin violations in the feature space.

Failure Boundaries

Points outside the margin street contribute zero gradient. Moving an outlier that is already strictly correctly classified does not alter the maximum margin hyperplane. Only active support vectors that lie on or violate the margin boundaries generate non-zero subgradients.
